# Frontend control and coverage inventory

This inventory records what the **frontend audit** exercised. The root agent's browser route/workflow results and the backend/attendance agents' integration checks are separate evidence and can close some of the gaps below. “Covered” means assertions actually ran in the 1,101-test suite or the added audit scripts; it does not mean every control was clicked against a real backend.

Existing role rendering tests cover super admin, entity admin, HR manager, zonal manager, branch manager, department head and employee. They also cover unassigned and unlinked accounts, combined grants, hidden screens and a manager's Employee view. Roles are access profiles; real-world **designations are a separate organization field**. Testing all roles does not establish all designation/department/branch combinations.
